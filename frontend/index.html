DATA GOVERNANCE

Methodology.

Understand the assumptions, uncertainties, and boundaries behind the numbers.

This is a demonstration, not a real investment recommendation.

The buildings, energy labels and maintenance conditions are fictional. Costs and screening scores are assumption-based and should not be used for procurement or compliance decisions.

01 / DATA

Reproducible source data

A seeded generator creates fictional public buildings and component observations. The pipeline cleans missing values, normalizes data and quarantines invalid records before loading SQLite.

— validated assets
02 / MODEL

Transparent cost assumptions

Buildings are linked to sites, systems, assets, inspections and modelled interventions. Quantities use asset-specific area or unit assumptions rather than treating floor area as the quantity for every component. These are scenario rates, not observed tender prices or trained machine-learning forecasts.

DKK · scenario estimates
03 / PRIORITY

Relative screening score

Priority is a 0–100 heuristic for comparing properties. High (≥70), medium (40–69), and low (<40) are illustrative cutoffs, not safety or legal classifications.

0–100 · heuristic index
04 / AI

Auditable question answering

Questions map to validated query plans. An optional LLM extracts intent only; a limited set of parameterized, read-only SQL templates retrieves the evidence.

SELECT only · no free-form SQL
